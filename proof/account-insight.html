Account thesis

Financially stable today.
Strategically vulnerable tomorrow.

The installed base is not collapsing. Several weak signals are converging before renewal: falling sentiment, delayed transformation, competitive activity, one unresolved incident, and uneven stakeholder coverage.

1ProtectStabilize delivery and resolve the incident.
2StrengthenRepair the Operations Director relationship.
3GrowPrioritize the most credible expansion plays.
4DefendUnderstand why the competitor entered the account.
5DemonstrateMake delivered value visible before renewal.

Management conclusion

Do not call the account healthy just because the €5M base is stable.Protect the installed relationship first, then strengthen stakeholder coverage, prove value, defend the account, and grow from evidence.
Decision readout

An executive briefing should tell a leader what is stable, what is changing, what could become expensive, and where intervention has leverage.

For (A) Group, core service delivery is mostly healthy and the €5M base is intact. Yet falling satisfaction, transformation delays, a weakening Operations Director relationship, an unresolved incident, and competitor consideration are converging. That makes the account financially stable today but strategically vulnerable tomorrow.

Why this is different from a CSDR

The CSDR asks “what happened in delivery?” The account brief asks “what does it mean for the relationship?”

ProtectDelivery + incident

Protect current trust before adding growth pressure.

StrengthenStakeholder coverage

Rebuild influence with the Operations Director.

DefendCompetitive position

Understand where the competitor is winning and where differentiation matters.

Account-management logic

Four decisions emerge from one integrated view.

1 · ProtectRemove the unresolved operational pain that can contaminate the renewal relationship.
2 · StrengthenMultithread beyond a strong COO relationship and repair the weakening operating relationship.
3 · GrowQualify the €1.25M pipeline and focus on opportunities whose customer case is strong.
4 · DefendTurn the competitor signal into a differentiation plan before an RFP forces reactive selling.
