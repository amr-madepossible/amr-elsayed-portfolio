Decision readout

The sample portfolio should not receive equal account-management attention. Four accounts have the combination of healthy usage and identifiable whitespace that supports a growth motion; three require protection of an existing relationship; three need enablement; and two require recovery attention. The segmentation exists to allocate scarce time against the next-best account action.

Analytical chain

Every step answers the question above.

1 · Establish the account objectiveFor each account, define whether the near-term objective is growth, protection, adoption, or recovery.
2 · Combine signal familiesAvoid making the decision from a single metric such as usage or satisfaction.
3 · Detect commercial whitespaceIdentify areas where customer value signals are strong but adoption / coverage is incomplete.
4 · Convert segment into motionAttach the segment to a concrete account plan, stakeholder action, and review cadence.
How this would be used

Analysis is only useful when it changes the next action.

This reconstructed sample is intentionally presented as a decision tool: define the business question, select the relevant signals, calculate or classify them consistently, isolate the material issue, and translate the finding into a clear action. A production model would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
