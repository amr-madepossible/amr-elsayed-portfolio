Executive takeaway

The sample avoids equal treatment across the portfolio. Four accounts merit growth motions, three need retention attention, three need enablement, and two need recovery. The analysis is valuable because it tells the account team where to spend time.

Analytical method

From raw signals to an accountable decision.

01 · SegmentationGroup accounts by the business action implied by their combined signals.
02 · ConcentrationAllocate effort toward signal strength and commercial relevance.
03 · WhitespaceFlag high-usage / low-coverage areas as potential growth signals.
04 · Next-best-actionTranslate each segment into a distinct account-management motion.
What this demonstrates

Senior-level operating judgment.

The sample is intentionally more rigorous than a presentation dashboard. It combines defined inputs, decision rules, exception handling, and a final action layer. A production version would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
