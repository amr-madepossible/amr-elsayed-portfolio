Executive takeaway

The sample 3.4x coverage headline looks healthy, but the exception layer shows where forecast confidence should be discounted. The operating model is designed to tell sellers what needs fixing, not just how much pipeline exists.

Analytical method

From raw signals to an accountable decision.

01 · Weighted forecastOpportunity value × stage probability, then discount unresolved exceptions.
02 · CoverageCompare pipeline capacity with the target rather than judging volume alone.
03 · AgeingFlag opportunities that exceed expected time in stage.
04 · Hygiene testsCheck stale dates, missing next steps, duplicate risk, and closed-but-open records.
What this demonstrates

Senior-level operating judgment.

The sample is intentionally more rigorous than a presentation dashboard. It combines defined inputs, decision rules, exception handling, and a final action layer. A production version would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
