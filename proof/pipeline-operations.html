Evidence confidence is a synthetic control score based on activity recency, close-date stability, next-step quality, decision-maker coverage, funding, and business-case evidence. It is not a second win probability.

Management conclusion

€1.25M of pipeline is not €1.25M of credible future business.The account has exactly 1.0× gross coverage against the expansion objective. Any material slippage creates a coverage gap.
Decision readout

The pipeline contains €1.25M of potential business, but that is only 1.0× the stated expansion ambition. Probability-weighting reduces the book to €715K, and evidence controls reduce the planning view further to €422K.

The difference is uncertainty. The €500K proposal has been stale for 45 days and slipped its close date three times; the €400K qualification opportunity lacks a decision maker, funding confirmation, and a complete business case. Those are forecast-quality issues, not simply stage labels.

The pipeline quality ladder

Volume → coverage → progression → velocity → aging → hygiene → forecast credibility.

1 · VolumeKnow the gross opportunity pool and the value represented.
2 · CoverageCompare pipeline to the €1.25M expansion objective. Here the book sits at exactly 1.0×.
3 · ProgressionCheck whether opportunities actually advance through stages rather than merely living there.
4 · Velocity and agingUse activity recency and close-date movement to detect stalls.
5 · HygieneInspect next steps, decision-maker coverage, funding, and business-case evidence.
6 · CredibilityReforecast after material exceptions have been inspected.
Connection to the account

The €500K opportunity is not an isolated sales problem.

The fictional transformation delay elsewhere in the account can directly suppress expansion timing. Pipeline operations must therefore remain connected to customer success and delivery signals.
