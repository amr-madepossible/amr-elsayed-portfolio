Decision readout

The synthetic record matches from CRM to contract, then diverges at downstream billing. Because the same $130K gap persists into landed revenue, the highest-value investigation is the ERP-side mapping or billing logic rather than the upstream commercial value. The control is designed to prove where the number changes before anyone applies a workaround.

Analytical chain

Every step answers the question above.

1 · Anchor the recordUse a stable commercial identifier to trace the same transaction across systems.
2 · Tie out each control pointCompare CRM, contract, ERP billing, and landed revenue in sequence.
3 · Localize the first divergenceThe first mismatch is the most useful root-cause boundary.
4 · Validate the correctionRequire evidence that the corrected mapping or billing result reconciles end to end.
How this would be used

Analysis is only useful when it changes the next action.

This reconstructed sample is intentionally presented as a decision tool: define the business question, select the relevant signals, calculate or classify them consistently, isolate the material issue, and translate the finding into a clear action. A production model would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
