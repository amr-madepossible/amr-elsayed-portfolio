Executive takeaway

The synthetic $130K gap is isolated between contract value and downstream billing / landed revenue. The control goes beyond “variance found” by creating a defensible route to diagnosis, correction, and verification.

Analytical method

From raw signals to an accountable decision.

01 · TraceabilityMatch the same commercial record across systems using stable identifiers.
02 · Variance bridgeSeparate timing, mapping, data, and commercial exceptions.
03 · Root causeRequire supporting evidence before closing an exception.
04 · Resolution controlTrack owner, corrective action, validation, and final status.
What this demonstrates

Senior-level operating judgment.

The sample is intentionally more rigorous than a presentation dashboard. It combines defined inputs, decision rules, exception handling, and a final action layer. A production version would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
