Executive takeaway

The synthetic backlog contains 19 cases, but only three are immediately SLA-sensitive. That distinction helps protect service levels while using recurring categories to identify the process issues worth fixing at source.

Analytical method

From raw signals to an accountable decision.

01 · SLA burnCompare age to the remaining SLA window instead of using age alone.
02 · Backlog segmentationBucket cases by category, owner, dependency, and age.
03 · Root-cause ParetoSeparate recurring categories from one-off exceptions.
04 · Control loopUse recurring causes to propose upstream process or SOP changes.
What this demonstrates

Senior-level operating judgment.

The sample is intentionally more rigorous than a presentation dashboard. It combines defined inputs, decision rules, exception handling, and a final action layer. A production version would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
