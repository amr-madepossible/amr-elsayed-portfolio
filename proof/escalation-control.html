Decision readout

Only three of the 19 synthetic cases are immediately SLA-sensitive, so treating the whole backlog as equally urgent would dilute response capacity. At the same time, six recurring root-cause classes indicate upstream process opportunities. The operating model therefore runs two loops in parallel: protect today's SLA, and remove the causes that create tomorrow's backlog.

Analytical chain

Every step answers the question above.

1 · Calculate SLA exposureCompare elapsed case age with the remaining SLA window.
2 · Protect the critical queuePrioritize the small number of cases that can materially breach service commitments.
3 · Classify recurrenceGroup cases by root cause, not just ticket type.
4 · Close the loop upstreamFeed recurring causes into SOP, training, workflow, or process-improvement actions.
How this would be used

Analysis is only useful when it changes the next action.

This reconstructed sample is intentionally presented as a decision tool: define the business question, select the relevant signals, calculate or classify them consistently, isolate the material issue, and translate the finding into a clear action. A production model would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
