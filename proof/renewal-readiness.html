Executive takeaway

At 78/100, the synthetic account is not automatically “safe.” Value realization is the largest weakness, so the highest-leverage action is to strengthen measurable outcome evidence before the renewal discussion.

Analytical method

From raw signals to an accountable decision.

01 · Readiness modelScore the evidence required for a strong renewal narrative.
02 · Gap analysisSeparate missing proof from genuine customer risk.
03 · Time horizonPrioritize evidence gaps based on the time left before renewal.
04 · Action planAssign one owner and one proof point to every material gap.
What this demonstrates

Senior-level operating judgment.

The sample is intentionally more rigorous than a presentation dashboard. It combines defined inputs, decision rules, exception handling, and a final action layer. A production version would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
