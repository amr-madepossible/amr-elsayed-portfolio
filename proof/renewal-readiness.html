Decision readout

A 78/100 readiness score does not mean “renewal safe.” The model says the account has a credible baseline, but value realization is the largest material weakness. With 45 days remaining, the logical move is to close the evidence gap, validate customer outcomes, and strengthen the renewal narrative before commercial negotiations intensify.

Analytical chain

Every step answers the question above.

1 · Define readinessSpecify what evidence must exist before an executive renewal conversation is considered ready.
2 · Score the evidenceWeight utilization, realized value, engagement, delivery quality, and commercial readiness.
3 · Prioritize evidence gapsFocus on the weakest factor that can materially change renewal confidence.
4 · Work to a deadlineConvert each gap into an owner, customer interaction, proof point, and due date.
How this would be used

Analysis is only useful when it changes the next action.

This reconstructed sample is intentionally presented as a decision tool: define the business question, select the relevant signals, calculate or classify them consistently, isolate the material issue, and translate the finding into a clear action. A production model would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
