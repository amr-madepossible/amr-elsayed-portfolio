Executive takeaway

The sample lands at 76/100. The useful conclusion is not simply “amber.” Success-criteria attainment is the largest weighted weakness, while adoption is improving. That points to an outcome-proof plan rather than a generic escalation.

Analytical method

From raw signals to an accountable decision.

01 · Score constructionTransparent weighted model across five signal families.
02 · Driver analysisSeparate the headline score from the reasons behind it.
03 · Trend testCompare current vs prior period to detect momentum, not just state.
04 · Action mappingMap the weakest material driver to one accountable next action.
What this demonstrates

Senior-level operating judgment.

The sample is intentionally more rigorous than a presentation dashboard. It combines defined inputs, decision rules, exception handling, and a final action layer. A production version would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
