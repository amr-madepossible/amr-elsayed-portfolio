Decision readout

The 76/100 headline is only useful when decomposed. Adoption is relatively strong and support pressure is improving, but success-criteria attainment is the largest weighted weakness. The priority is therefore to close the outcome-evidence gap before the 45-day renewal horizon, not to launch a generic “customer health” intervention.

Analytical chain

Every step answers the question above.

1 · Define outcome riskTranslate the account plan into explicit success criteria that can be scored.
2 · Weight what mattersWeight the drivers according to their relevance to customer value and renewal readiness.
3 · Diagnose the weakest material driverIdentify the gap that is both weak and commercially consequential.
4 · Assign the interventionConvert the diagnosis into one accountable action, owner, evidence point, and review date.
How this would be used

Analysis is only useful when it changes the next action.

This reconstructed sample is intentionally presented as a decision tool: define the business question, select the relevant signals, calculate or classify them consistently, isolate the material issue, and translate the finding into a clear action. A production model would use validated source data, documented metric definitions, and the relevant customer or internal governance process.
