Management conclusion

The account is not failing. Several parts of the value chain are.Fix delivery ownership where the provider caused the delay, engage the customer where dependency is theirs, run an adoption intervention, and close the evidence gap around value.
Decision readout

The headline numbers tell a mixed story. Contractual delivery is broadly healthy at 92%, but transformation performance is 18 points below target, new-service adoption is 19 points below target, and satisfaction has fallen from 4.5 to 3.8. The analytical mistake would be to treat those as one problem.

The two transformation delays have different causes: one is an internal missed deliverable and therefore needs delivery ownership; the other is blocked by customer data and therefore needs sponsor engagement. That distinction changes the action.

Adoption diagnosis

51% adoption is a behavior and enablement problem before it is a service-design problem.

Barrier 01Training

Users have not received enough practical enablement to make the new service part of daily work.

Barrier 02Process clarity

Some teams do not understand where the new service fits into the operating workflow.

Barrier 03Rollout coverage

One business unit has not completed rollout, suppressing adoption breadth.

Value realization

Usage is evidence of activity. Outcome improvement is evidence of value.

The fictional example moves average processing time from 7.0 days to 4.5 days, a 35.7% reduction. That is stronger evidence than saying the customer “uses the service.” The 68% value-realization result therefore points to an evidence gap across the full outcome set, not proof that the service itself is failing.

Action architecture

The CSDR ends with owners, not observations.

1 · Internal delayEscalate delivery ownership, recover the missed deliverable, and reset the milestone plan.
2 · Customer dependencyEngage the project sponsor and secure the missing data through a dated dependency plan.
3 · Adoption gapLaunch targeted enablement and track adoption by business unit.
4 · Value proofDocument the processing-time improvement and validate the remaining outcomes with the customer.
